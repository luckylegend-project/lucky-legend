# Lucky Legend Roadmap

The Lucky Legend roadmap is intentionally simple.

It does not focus on rapid expansion or short term objectives.

Instead, it follows a long term approach built on continuity, transparency, and presence.

Progress is measured through consistency over time, not speed.

---

## Phase 1

### Foundation

* Token creation on Solana
* Fair launch through Pump.fun
* Initial distribution
* Establishment of the project's core structure

---

## Phase 2

### Transparency

* Official website
* Public GitHub repository
* Public documentation
* Whitepaper publication
* On-chain verification
* Public token information

---

## Phase 3

### Presence

* Organic discovery
* Consistent communication
* Development of a recognizable project identity
* Long term public presence

---

## Phase 4

### Continuity

* Ongoing refinement
* Continued transparency
* Documentation maintenance
* Sustainable project development

---

## Phase 5

### Long Term Presence

* Continued existence over time
* Independent operation
* No forced expansion
* No artificial growth strategies

---

## Principles

* Transparency over promises
* Consistency over speed
* Presence over pressure
* Continuity over hype

---

Lucky Legend is not designed to rush from one phase to the next.

Its purpose is to remain present, visible, and consistent over time.
